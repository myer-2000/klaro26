# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Klaro26 to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of experience level or background.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent; give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what's best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers. All
reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove
comments, commits, code, and contributions that violate this Code of Conduct,
and may ban contributors for repeated or severe violations.

This Code of Conduct is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
